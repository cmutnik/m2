About M2

We started M2 because growing businesses deserve HR support that's as thoughtful and ambitious as they are.

Our Story

Built on real HR experience, not theory.

M2 HR Consulting was founded to close the gap between the HR support large companies take for granted and what small and mid-sized businesses can typically access. After years spent building HR functions from the ground up, our founders saw the same challenges repeat across industries: unclear policies, inconsistent hiring, and leaders without the tools to manage people well.

Today, M2 partners with founders, executives, and HR teams to bring structure, compassion, and compliance to the way organizations manage their people. We're not hired to be your HR department — through training and hands-on advising, we make sure the HR you run is done correctly and legally.

Our Mission

People-first HR that scales with your business.

We believe strong HR practices aren't just about avoiding risk — they're a genuine driver of business performance. Our mission is to give every client the HR foundation they need to attract great people, keep them engaged, and grow with confidence.
